THE STORY

The city's power plant went dark. VOLT-7, a small courier robot with an energy blade and a parcel on its back, is sent down the maintenance line with one last delivery: power. Explore 15 connected sectors, from the cooling towers to the reactor core. Find upgrades that open new paths, and shut down the machines that took over the plant.

FEATURES

4 movement abilities
Double jump, wall-climb, dash and grapple. Each one opens sealed paths in places you have already been.
3 weapons
Energy blade, then a long arc-whip, then a plasma launcher. Blue barriers and red plating only give way to the right weapon.
6 enemies + 2 bosses
Sparkbugs, volt drones, turrets, spring hoppers, shield sentries and static mines. Then The Foreman and the Core Warden.
Tight controls
Coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump height, pogo down-attack, and you can slash enemy bolts out of the air.
Map and saves
A schematic map marks rooms, save stations, items you have not collected and bosses. Your progress saves automatically.
100% code-made
All the pixel art, the font, the sound effects and the chiptune music are generated in GDScript. The game has no image or audio files.
